A traditional formula to contemplate  
You may also repeatedly reflect on the standard recollection formulas found across the Nikāyas:  
  
• Recollection of the Buddha:  
“Iti pi so Bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho...”  
“Such indeed is the Blessed One: worthy, perfectly awakened...”  
• Recollection of the Dhamma:  
“Svākkhāto Bhagavatā Dhammo...”  
“The Dhamma is well taught by the Blessed One...”  
• Recollection of the Sangha:  
“Supaṭipanno Bhagavato sāvaka-saṅgho...”  
“The Blessed One’s Sangha of disciples has practiced well...”

A simple contemplation pattern  
• Read one short sutta slowly.  
• Pause on one quality of the Buddha, Dhamma, or Sangha.  
• Ask: “How does this quality appear in lived practice?”  
• Let confidence become ethical action: speech, restraint, generosity, mindfulness.
