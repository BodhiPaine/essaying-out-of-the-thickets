I cannot overstate the significance of this insight for me, that one can attend (in)directly to the *origin of experience*, that we can habitually *inhabit our direct experience* rather than habitually perceiving it through conditioning, which is essentially the definition of #ignorance. Combining Frankl's idea of the 'space between' with Buddha's teaching about #yoniso-manasikara inspires confidence that one can dwell in that space (of equanimity, I presume) Frankl described. Such an insight may remain buried here until I know where to put it. At least, I can say with confidence that [[07. What the Buddha Taught, and Didn't Teach#Wise Attention, *yoniso manasikara*|yoniso manasikara]] necessarily ranks at the top of Buddha's [[Essential Suttas#'Wise Attention, 'Yoniso Manasikara'|essential teachings]].
