# About 'Me'
As good a place to start as any is with 心, which is more 'me' than anything else. I checked in with the idea for a writing prompt, and found this [nice but anonymous post](https://lalala-nihon.com/blog-articles/kokoro) by that surveys the very broad and deep relevance of this humble[^1] little character. 

The character means *kokoro*, and in Japanese culture it stretches back thousands of years and connects, unsurprisingly, with Buddhism. (The chronology here might be a bit spurious, because I don't think Buddhism had met Japan thousands of years ago ~ it had to travel there from ancient India through China over at least hundreds of years, so it might be more accurate to place it's connection with Buddhism at around a thousand years ago, but caveat: that's a half-informed guess.) But whenever, and since then, it has come to span so many domains of the human condition that I might say it has a laundry list of applications.
