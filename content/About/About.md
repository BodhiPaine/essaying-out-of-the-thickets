# About 'Me'
As good a place to start as any is with 心, which is more 'me' than anything else. I checked in with the idea for a writing prompt, and found this nice post that surveys the very broad and deep relevance of this humble[^1] little character. 

The character means *kokoro*, and in Japanese culture it stretches back thousands of years and connects, unsurprisingly, with Buddhism. But more, and since then, it has come to span so many domains of the human condition that I might say it has a laundry list of applications.
