*Arms Raised with a V* is an experiment in [[digital gardening]], non-linear writing-as-thinking, and reflecting-in-public. I have been playing with publishing my half-finished ideas online since about 2008 when I started my first blog, *Socratic Ignorance is Bliss* and, reflecting on my researching into #digital-gardening just now I realised that for at least as long as that I have been interested developing a personal knowledge base ~ at the time I was setting up that first blog, I was also looking into personal wikis, but they felt cumbersome and looked unsightly, and Wordpress was around by then, so I got into blogging instead. Reading Maggie Appleton's page about the history of digital gardening helped me understand that I am not alone in my disillusionment with (reverse-)chronological order and the pressure to think first, publish later in the spirit of polished essay-style posts that are, worse, pathologically linear. 

I am not sure where or how I am going to publish this yet, but I have started looking at Logseq, which so far has a pretty #interface but a daunting-sounding name.
