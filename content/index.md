This corner of the internet is an experiment in long-form [[About#About Digital Gardening|digital gardening]], recently seeded with some [[About#About "Don't Believe What I Think"|essaying about faith in Buddhism]] and potentially a site for transplanting some essayed subjects. 

Digital gardening is a form of non-linear hypertext narrative that I have been wanting to find for ~15 years, popularised by %%\[Maggie Appleton?]%%. There is a primary narrative thread here in *[[00. Introduction|Don't Believe What I Think]]*, and then sprawling sub-branches I have started calling Driftwood and might stash behind the shed later.
