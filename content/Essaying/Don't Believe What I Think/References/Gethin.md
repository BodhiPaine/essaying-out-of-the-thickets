# 110, faith
The first indexed entry about #faith seems to be describing 'affection' or 'religious emotion' ( #pema) rather than the 'reasoned conviction' of #saddhā. 

Then going into page 111 he describes Melford Spiro's anthropological observations of post-war Burmese Buddhism: 
- i) apotropaic; 
- ii) kammatic; and 
- iii) nibbānic, 

where 
- i) is about mundane/daily 'protective' practices and the improvement of immediate worldly conditions; 
- ii) is about making merit to secure a better rebirth[^1]; and 
- iii) practice aimed directly at nibbāna. 

Spiro said 
- i) magic, amulates, chanting; 
- ii) *dāna* and *sīla* (with *bhāvana* noteably absent from [[the classic bases of merit]]); and 
- iii) meditation (there's the *bhāvana*!)
