The first that comes to mind is whether [[07. What the Buddha Taught, and Didn't Teach#Full Awakening, or Liberation from Suffering 🎯|awakening is possible ✅]], and I am going to give them each a little badge as I go, because this is my working manual more than anything. I choose to believe this, in a form of [[Pascal’s Wager]] that feels a lot more like the saying, 'Aim for the stars - if you fail, you will still land on the moon.'

I worried sometimes that my inherited worldview was hindering my practice, but it turns out my worldview is a lot more Buddhist than I thought - the only major hurdle is the idea of 'progress' or [[05. Tripping Over Worldviews#History as Linear Progress|linear history]], and the fantasy-consolation of humans evolving consciousness to an increasingly refined state, which is a very Enlightenment thing for me to think I suppose, but not an idea I necessarily agree with.

I was worried about feeling or being perceived as credulous, but I understand that *saddhā* is like water to the oil of credulity. As [[01. The Nature of Doubt#^soma-credulity|Soma says]], 'Study of a teaching through inquiring into it is unfavourable to credulity.'

One of the main sources of doubt for me over the last few years, since moving from the Mahayana tradition to the Theravada, is [[01. The Nature of Doubt#The Bodhisattva Ideal|the bodhisattva ideal]] and how this dove-tails so nicely with my [[01. The Nature of Doubt#I Should be 'Helping'|perceived need to save the world]]: a bodhisattva is the 'bleeding-hearted liberal' *par excellence*, the ultimate social-justice warrior. These are terms I do not use disparagingly - they are self-descriptors. 

There is a convincing case though anyway, that the idea of a multiple-lifetime 'bodhisattva career' is an interpretation of Buddhist cosmology that is not found in Early Buddhism - it was *added* to the cosmology later. 

People are putting 'Bodhisattva' on their business cards while in the cosmology, such an aspirant needs to make the vow in the presence of living buddha, who then has to prophecy whether the aspirant will experience the right conditions. So it's not going to happen until Matreya (the next buddha, not the hippy festival), and then it will be for one buddha only - there can be only one budda per world-system. 

Despite knowing all this, I still sometimes worry that the practice is [[01. The Nature of Doubt#Self-awakening is Selfish|selfish]] because I have these conditioned beliefs that I need to 'help' in a 'more direct' way or [[07. What the Buddha Taught, and Didn't Teach#Can Dhamma Save the World?|save the world]]. I can't quite tick these because they are still quite gnarly sources of doubt for me 🚩👀 .
