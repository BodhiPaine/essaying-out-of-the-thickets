**Don't Believe What I Think:**
Essaying on having *saddhā* in faith

🎯 Perhaps my first encounter with Buddhist faith (*saddhā*) was at a Tibetan centre in Adelaide, inquiring about how to hold [[07. What the Buddha Taught#Skilful View,* sammā-diṭṭhi*|skilful view]] when I had not yet had direct experience of the truth myself – and being told I needed to take it on faith. This didn’t exactly inspire ~confidence~ in me at the time, but there are seven other [[07. What the Buddha Taught#the Eightfold Path|Path factors]] to consider and so I focused on those. In fact, I think that is part of the advice I got: focus on the other Path factors, and faith in skilful view will naturally develop.^80ca36

However, a wheel with a single untended spoke is bound to yield a bumpy ride at best, and so it was: not looking into right view because I thought it could wait until later in my development, I left untended a wide range of half-(un)conscious [[01. The Nature of Doubt|doubts]] that would rear their head from time to time. And by ‘wide’ I mean ‘cosmological’: is [[07. What the Buddha Taught#Full Awakening, or Liberation from Suffering 🎯|enlightenment]] in this lifetime within the range of my worldview? If not, then doubt. If so, then striving. But I'm getting ahead of myself. #cosmology 

My interest was aroused again when I was at Bodhivana[^7], where I found [['Transcendental Dependent Arising'|Bhikkhu Bodhi's essay]] about [[08. Dependent Liberation|dependent liberation]], and it was faith again that I got stuck on. All I could find about cultivating faith was contemplation of the Triple Gem, which just felt a bit circular for my tastes - like reading the Bible for evidence it is God's word. 

When I was at Dhammagiri after Bodhivana, I started hearing about the idea of the *dhammanusari* and *saddhanusari*, the wisdom- or faith-follower - one who has developed the [[04. The Five Faculties|the five faculties]] to an unspecified degree of maturity. It is said%%find suttas%% that such a practitioner was destined at least for [[07.03. Full Awakening, or Liberation from Suffering 🎯#Stream-entry The first stage of awakening|stream-entry]] at the time of death - and, fancifully, that a world-system could not end while one remained a *dhammanusari* or *saddhanusari*. As well as these guarantees, it is in the suttas %%somewhere%% that developing the faculties to this extent was considered by the Buddha to be a kind of pre-noble noble one, or or pre-==*ariyasāvaka*==.

I love it when I find new stones on the Path, and ones that might even be within reach. So I was intrigued, and this was perhaps the tipping point, after which I could hardly prevent myself from looking more into faith. Starting with [[04. The Five Faculties|the five faculties]], it soon became clear that faith is central to progress on the Path in general, but specifically for these early stages: one must fully develop faith among the faculties [[07.03. Full Awakening, or Liberation from Suffering 🎯#*Dhammanusari* and *Saddhanusari*|to become a -nusari]]%%Pali for this class generally%%, and from there one may progress to [[07.03. Full Awakening, or Liberation from Suffering 🎯#Stream-entry The first stage of awakening|the path of stream-entry]], the fruit of which dispels all doubt about the Path - along with the other first three fetters.%%do I need to write about these? consider whether I can take it on faith that these are reasonably considered fetters%%

The same as I wasn't keen on faith at the Tibetan centre in Adelaide, I knew that I would need to do some work on understanding *saddhā* if I wanted to cultivate it. I needed to know more about what faith actually means in Buddhism, and what it doesn't mean.

So if _saddhābhāvanā_ is a genuine Pali word, I can say this garden is an exercise in _saddhābhāvanā_ - in cultivating faith. I am building the essay for this garden because even though my _saddhā_ is generally sufficiently robust, I understand it may wane at any time[^9], so having a resource such as this - and having gone through such a process to build it - will come in handy.

My understanding of faith began to feel increasingly pressing as my study developed, because I began to see how compromised faith was impacting the [[00.01. Faith and Effort|amount and type of effort 👉🏻]] that I was applying.
