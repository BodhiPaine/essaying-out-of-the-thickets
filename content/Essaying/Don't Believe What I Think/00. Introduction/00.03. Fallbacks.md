I decided my curiosity about [[06. Cultivating Saddhā#Developing the Mind|the potential of the human mind]] is reason enough to pursue this lifestyle.

It helps to think of my training as an #apprenticeship or a university degree, and to consider it worthwhile to get some meaningful training at least, before I resume trying to help others - this depends on faith in the oxygen mask analogy, which doesn't need a note because everyone knows.

~~

Having articulated now this litany of surfaced doubts that were holding me back in practice, and fallbacks that I can lean-into, it is a heartfelt segue to something I realised only later in the essaying process: a large part of my motivation to pursue Buddhist practice is not so much faith in Buddhism, but from a long-time [[00.04. Disillusionment|crisis of faith 👉🏻]] in my own inherited worldview. No matter how shaky my *saddhā* might be, my faith in hedonistic materialism is shakier ~ which makes this one of my fallbacks itself.

It's kind of like the parable of the trapeze: having let go of any faith I might have once had in the mainstream worldview, I am sailing through the air in search of something to grasp about Buddhism.
