The state of my faith ... an #epistemic-state? ... determines the extent, nature, and direction of my effort. The classic example is about going to the park: one would not set out if they weren’t convinced they could make it and that doing so would be worthwhile. 

If I don't believe it is possible to become [[07. What the Buddha Taught, and Didn't Teach#Full Awakening, or Liberation from Suffering 🎯|awakened]] in this lifetime, or that such awakening will benefit others, I might 'hedge my bets', splitting my time between *bhāvanā* and social work - or between *bhāvanā* and writing a book, or endlessly fiddling with this garden essay. I am more likely to try helping in ways that are 'more direct', not in ways reliant upon obscure [[07. What the Buddha Taught, and Didn't Teach#Metaphysics|metaphysical mechanisms]] one might never understand (cf. [[AN 10.95, Uttiya Sutta, ‘With Uttiya’|AN 10.95]], Uttiya Sutta).

Depending on my worldview, it sometimes comes up that achieving a literary goal (or even just the process of writing) will make me happy, or that I have only this life, so I need to make a contribution before I go through some form of social work. As much as I can say honestly I don't think the former will genuinely make me happy, or that the latter is true - I can know this objectively, but sometimes the thoughts come up in meditation, so having a place to work through reflections on that #conditioning will be helpful (to me). 

If I believe that working on my own spiritual development over many lifetimes is possible, I may be less pulled toward social work and more confident my #agency resides primarily in developing an increasingly purified, refined mind and heart. This will naturally impact the choices I make about the extent, nature, and direction of my #effort.

As well as being concerned about how compromised faith was impacting my effort, there were other doubts that would surface now and then, and which I wanted to essay my through - so the essaying has been an exercise in [[00.02. Surfacing Doubts|surfacing those doubts 👉🏻]] to have a better look at them.
