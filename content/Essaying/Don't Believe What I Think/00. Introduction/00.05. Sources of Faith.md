# Dhamma Bridges
Finding [[06. Cultivating Saddhā#Dhamma Bridges|intersections between dhamma and Western psychology]] always buoys my faith - these are disciplines I already have some understanding of, and often they express their insights in my language, from a shared cultural background. With an evolving understanding of how to discern dhamma from *adhamma*, I can look at these disciplines and modalities and consider for myself whether their teachings are [[07. What the Buddha Taught, and Didn't Teach#What is Dhamma, and aDhamma?|dhamma-aligned]] or not. The Buddha didn't claim a monopoly on truth, and many practitioners of numerous different traditions altogether are coming at the same conclusions, along with contemporary scientists.
