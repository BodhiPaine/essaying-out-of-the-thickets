A well-developed mind does not hold  fixed views, as exemplified by this favourite quote - from Bertrand Russell: 

> The essence of the Liberal outlook lies not in what opinions are held, but in how they are held: instead of being held dogmatically, they are held tentatively, and with a consciousness that new evidence may at any moment lead to their abandonment. 

Other qualities include: 

- high tendency for thoughts to be wholesome, such as characterised by the brahmaviharas; 
- strong capacity for continuous mindfulness; 
- strong capacity for concentration, attention, memory, etc. ...

A well-developed mind can access stillness, calm, collectedness, at will.

Loevinger recognises ambiguity tolerance as a quality of a mind that is at E7-8 on the 9-point scale of mental development. Other qualities include: 

- metacognition / meta-awareness
- capacity for interoception - 
	- and the attendant ability to recognise the state of one's nervous system and regulate, which requires a certain amount of knowledge;
	- and the attendant capacity to detect *vedana*
- capacity for introspection
