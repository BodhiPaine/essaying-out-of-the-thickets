... as well as the question of whether there is a difference between saving the world and solving all your problems[^1], there is the question of whether the world needs saving in the first place. If it does, perhaps it needs saving from us. Or, as I understand now from the [[#AN 10.95 *Uttiyasutta*, ‘With Uttiya’|sutta]] discussed below, it is actually that *we need saving from the world*.

What I mean though, is that I have always felt a need to ‘save’ or ‘change’ the world ~ at least, for as long as I can remember ~ and it is only recently that I have started to wonder whether this ‘need’ is actually more like a desire, and a conceited one at that. `More to come on that note,` but for now I am interested to have found a sutta where Uttiya asks the Buddha, ‘is the whole world saved, or half, or a third’ by the teachings about the cessation of suffering, which was the Buddha’s core preoccupation, not the sort of [[the Ten Unprofitable Questions|metaphysical enquiry]] proposed by Uttiya.
