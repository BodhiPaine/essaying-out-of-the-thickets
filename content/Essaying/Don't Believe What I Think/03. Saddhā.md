*Saddhā* has a central place in both the five faculties and dependent liberation. Without faith we wouldn't have the motivation to strive - we would have no reason to apply effort or persist[^1] in the practice

[^1]: Thān translates *viriya* as 'persistence', which I prefer to 'energy' ... it is easy to apply effort when one has energy, but the importance is in applying effort even when one doesn't have the energy or motivation: that is persistence.
