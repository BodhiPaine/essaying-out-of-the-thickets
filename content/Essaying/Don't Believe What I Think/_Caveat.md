One very-important caveat to understand upfront: I don't want to teach you Buddhism; what I am doing here is more about articulating my own understanding, as a process of writing- and learning-in-public. If you happen to gain some understanding from that then honestly, that is secondary. 

I am certainly not making any truth claims here about Buddhism or anything else - I am including updates to 'epistemic status' as much as possible, to be clear on what I understand to be the truth, but everything here is provisional, until it isn't.
