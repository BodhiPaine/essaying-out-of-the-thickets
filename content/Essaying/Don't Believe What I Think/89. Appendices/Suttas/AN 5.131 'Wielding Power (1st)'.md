Buddha taught the five qualities of a wheel-turning monarch, still relevant today for heads of #government, who 'wields power only in a principled manner', and he taught the dhamma using the same five qualities: ([Sujato](https://suttacentral.now/an5.131/en/sujato)) He 

- knows what is right, 
- knows principle, 
- knows moderation, 
- knows the right time, and 
- knows the assembly.

Assembly, perhaps is about knowing which dhamma to teach when, and to whom.
