# SN 12.23 The Upanisa Sutta, 'Proximate Cause'
In the [Upanisa Sutta](https://suttacentral.net/sn12.23/en/sujato), about which Bhikkhu Bodhi wrote [a brilliant essay](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/lib/authors/bodhi/wheel277.html) called 'Transcendental Dependent Arising', the Buddha presents a classic formula of dependent arising (or dependent origination) as a chain of causality for suffering ~ followed by a less-known formula of *transcendental* dependent arising (or dependent liberation), which is *a set of instructions* for *how to transcend suffering*. #dependent-liberation
# SN 48.50 Āpaṇa Sutta, 'At Āpaṇa'
Translated as the [Saddhā Sutta](https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/SN/SN48_50.html) by Thanissaro, this discourse presents the Five Faculties as cyclical or graduated/gradual, and beginning with faith: faith begets %%conditions?%% energy, which begets mindfulness ... concentration ... wisdom, and I get from reading this sutta that: a) one can expect insight to arise somewhat naturally from _samādhi_, though of course we shouldn’t neglect wisdom practices, and b) the cycle returns to faith after wisdom, completing (and re-starting) the cycle as one’s faith inspires effort, again and again, etc, so the Faculties are gradual in that one conditions the next/others, and cyclical in that the ‘fifth’ faculty, wisdom, inspires faith, the ‘first’ faculty, although like all of these things they are not as linear as ‘first’ and ‘fifth’ would suggest.
