---
The cosmology of Buddhism suggests that the one-world we know (this Earth, on this current timeline, with all its history and evolution) is just one-world-of-many among innumerable and beginningless world-systems in boundless space. It could be said, from this panoramic perspective, that we do in fact have a planet B - or, rather, we have so many planets that no alphabet could contain a representation of their abundance.

This perspective might be considered by some a slippery slope into 'quietism', and could certainly become a form of spiritual bypassing, but it supports my #faith because it moves me out of the paradigm that I have this one life, the one-shot dress rehearsal.
