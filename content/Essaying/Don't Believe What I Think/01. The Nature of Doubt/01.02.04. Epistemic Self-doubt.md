Related to doubting one's [[01.02.03. Self-efficacy|self-efficacy]], often shows up like, *Even if liberation is possible, do I have the grit to apply myself? Do I have faith in myself? Am I confident I can achieve the highest potential of these instructions?* in particular, this kind of doubt is 

> the special case where *what we doubt is our ability* to achieve an epistemically favorable state, for example, to achieve [[justified true belief|true beliefs]]. 

If this doesn't come up for the average *==putthujana==* now and then, they are not a patthujana.
