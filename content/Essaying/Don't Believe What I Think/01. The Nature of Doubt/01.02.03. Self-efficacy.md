*Can I do this? Am I doing the meditation right? Should I move to a different object? Should I stand on one leg?* Etcetera, etcetera ... 

As we develop [[07. What the Buddha Taught#Wise Attention, *Yoniso Manasikara*|wise attention]], it becomes easier to discern from the 'sign of the mind' whether our meditation is working - improving concentration, reducing defilements - and how long to persist or when to change.

There is not much to say here, except that sometimes I have these doubts, but I am confident about working with them ✅.
