With websites like [Sutta Central](https://suttacentral.now), [Pāli Canon Study Tool](https://study.jhana.info), and [The Buddha's Words](https://www.thebuddhaswords.net/home/index.html), we have unprecedented access to the whole Pali Canon, the early teachings of the Buddha.
