An edge is anywhere I know there is a Path factor but I can't seem to 'hold my seat' in this area of practice - there is motivation lacking, from a faith leak essentially. For example, I might find it difficult to refrain fromo the 'outgoing exuberance' of expressing myself through idle chatter. Or I need some inspiration to motivate my training in restraint around food or thoughts of women, landscapes, and books.

%%This is hearsay but I have heard%% the Buddha said if we had another desire as great as that for food and procreation, it would be impossible to realise [[enlightenment]]. I find that inspiring because it helps to zoom out and see ourselves at a time in a long history of evolution and conditioning - beyond the evolution of this world-system, we also have the weight of kamma from countless lifetimes prior to this one. It takes the sting out of making a mess of things because one doesn't remember all the dress rehearsals. 

If one day I am especially plagued by cravings in one or all of these domains, I will try to not feel disheartened - or *Be disheartened, but don't give up*. I don't know where that quote is from except it was stitched onto a RARE bag I had for a while. I like that it permits the feeling of being disheartened because that shouldn't be ignored. The Path is there to be walked as well as we can - it's not a test, we just need to know where our #edge is.
