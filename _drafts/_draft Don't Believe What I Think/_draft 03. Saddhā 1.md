

# Protecting the Truth

%%I appreciate this about the Buddha%%, and I suppose he knew (as he stated many times) that by teaching the truth of the end of suffering he necessarily wasn't teaching, or making claims about, other truths, such as metaphysical truths. Knowing the limitations of [[07. What the Buddha Taught, and Didn't Teach|what the Buddha taught]] is going to support one's practice if one is curious about things like #metaphysics - it will help to know which curiosities do not lead to #liberation. One such curio: 

> [!How does Buddhist meditation create change in society?[^2]]
> the mechanics of how one's bhavana influences change in society - by modelling an alternative way of being?, or by somehow influencing the collective consciousness? or both. Is one of these ways more or less direct than the other? Do we need to know *how* it works, or is it enough to have *saddhā* that our mental training is for the benefit of gods and humans.

It has been helpful for me to begin considering whether it is conceited to think one needs to *change the world*? There are lots of doom-claims out there and it seems anthropogenic climate change is a real threat that may cause human extinction, but the worldly ways of averting this are not getting very far because we are demanding or expecting change without having first prioritised changing our own mind and heart. Does changing *that* world, the fathom-long world, change [[AN 10.95, Uttiya Sutta, ‘With Uttiya’|the whole world, or a third]]? Shouldn't we get our own house in order, apply the oxygen mask to ourselves? Buddha taught liberation from personal suffering as a means of alleviating collective suffering, by 'supporting the welfare of gods and humans'. 

Meanwhile, the pursuit of [[07. What the Buddha Taught, and Didn't Teach#The Ten Unprofitable Questions|unprofitable questions]] creates a 'wilderness of opinions', which I suppose is the same as the [[thicket of views]]. 

[^2]: A source of doubt that can creep in easily if one is not clear on how getting one's own house in order first is always the priority, the way of supporting the welfare of gods and humans.
