# incomparable tamer of men
Buddha, My Refuge has 6.8 (pp. 85-86)

the example of Angulimala depicted here does not show how/why Buddha was incomparable in taming. He mentions that he has stopped killing - in the context of being asked to stop walking. I mean, he puns on 'stop' when Angu asks him to stand still, and Angu seems to think this is so clever that he vows to stop killing and become a disciple. It just doesn't describe any special quality that I can call to mind in contemplation - it is depicted as though it happened by magic, like in a dime novel
