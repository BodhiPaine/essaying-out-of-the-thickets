%%some Pali and other details, including a sutta reference, for when it becomes relevant%%
    
    - faith in the Triple Gem (_ratanattāya-saddhā_[1](#sdfootnote1sym))
        

- confidence in the Buddha
    
    - his enlightenment
        
    - his qualities
        
- confidence in the Dhamma
    
    - in the four factors of stream-entry
        
    - in particular, Right View
        
        - kamma (_kammaphala-saddhā_[2](#sdfootnote2sym)), rebirth, dependent origination
            
- confidence in the Sangha
    

  

- _viriyindriya_ (energy)
    
    - energy for abandoning unwholesome states and cultivating wholesome states #SkillfulEffort
        

  

- _satindriya_ (mindfulness, remembering, recollecting)
    
    - one is mindful, ‘possessing supreme mindfulness and discretion, one who remembers and recollects what was done and said long ago’ (Bodhi)
        

  

- _samādhindriya_ (concentration, collectedness)
    
    - quite secluded from _kāma_ and unwholesome states, one enters and dwells in the first _jhāna_ … and onward
        

  

- _paññindriya_ (wisdom)
    
    - **SN48:50** discerning/understanding that _sa__ṁ__sara_ is beginningless, with beings wandering, hindered by ignorance and fettered by craving, but that cessation is possible
